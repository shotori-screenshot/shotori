## Toolbar

- Remove the horizontal scrollbar from overlay and floating-pin toolbar rows so fractional display scales (e.g. `1.25x`, `1.5x`) no longer render a spurious slider bar along the bottom of the toolbar
- Keep toolbar rows strictly single-line and move trailing controls that exceed narrow display widths into a `...` overflow popover, preserving edge drag grips and inline text-editing focus
