## Fixes

- Annotation editing state survives tool switches and menu actions
- Toolbar annotation colors apply to the selected mark or live text and preserve undo history; text outline and background pickers sit beside their toggles with padding and corner radius in a popover; both annotation rows stay within display bounds
- The eraser records erasures on the marks they erase (issue #30): one undo step per gesture and a partially erased stroke stays a stroke instead of an independent pixel mask

## Performance

- Eraser gestures paint incrementally over the cached frame — 0.5 ms per pointer move at 1080p and 2.0 ms at 4k, down from 114–167 ms full-scene replays that read as a multi-second stall; finishing an erasure on a long stroke costs 3.5 ms instead of 4.6 s
- Dragging a selected shape replays over a split plane: ~10 ms per frame at 1080p whether or not a magnifier is on screen, down from 20–22 ms plain and 65–68 ms with a magnifier, and no longer queues a background render per drag frame

## Validation

558 tests pass, with 3 existing ignored tests. Formatting, default and perf Clippy checks, and release builds pass. The eraser and drag preview paths carry bit-exactness tests against the full rasterization pipeline, plus `--bench erase` / `--bench move` regression gates.
