## New annotation tools

- Magnifiers with separate source and enlarged regions, 1–8× zoom, connector styles, annotation sampling, antialiasing and optional shadows
- Rectangular and elliptical spotlights with shared background dimming
- Editable curves for lines and arrows, seven arrow silhouettes and five stroke patterns where supported
- Rectangle and brush modes for mosaic, blur and local erasure

## Editing and selection

- Marquee and Shift multi-selection, group movement and deletion, with undo/redo
- Shape fill, rotation, rectangle corner radius, ellipse sectors and arcs
- Installed fonts, bold and italic text, outlines and backgrounds, padding, rounded corners and configurable wrapping
- Decimal, alphabetic and Roman sequence labels, editable values and attached arrows
- Icon dropdowns for mutually exclusive options, independent shape controls and consistent selection while changing parameters
- Escape dismisses the active interaction in stages; right-click completes a polyline before leaving drawing mode

## Fixes

- Restore visible text carets, pointer placement and drag selection, including decorated text and fractional display scaling
- Font changes apply to selected text and preserve its selection; font-menu keyboard and pointer events no longer interfere with the canvas
- Keep text and selection geometry aligned during movement; update magnifiers from live annotation drafts
- Preserve other annotations during magnifier updates and avoid stale spotlight frames
- Keep pin glow on the focused pin surface

## Validation and scope

546 tests pass, with 3 existing ignored tests. Formatting, default and perf Clippy checks, and release builds pass. Isolated Wayland rendering checks cover representative editing and compositing interactions at fractional scaling.

This release expands regular annotations; it does not claim complete PixPin parity. Watermarks and model-based smart erasure are not included. See the [annotation capability matrix](https://github.com/shotori-screenshot/shotori/blob/v0.17.0/docs/annotation-parity.md) for remaining work. Long-capture stitching remains experimental.
